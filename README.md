# Unity-Game
This is a game written in C# and Unity.

To view a demo of the game played visit - https://drive.google.com/file/d/1X6gQoL-UWbY6qHqxaONbsq4NaW0jrn4I/view?usp=sharing

The goal of this game is to accomplish two challenges- 1. Save the daughter who has been taken to an underground cellar and fight the bad guys holding her. 2. Help a local chef fetch her ingredients.

How to Play:
Use the arrows to go left, right, forward, and back. Use your mouse to direct your player. To throw bombs press the G button. To use your sword press the H button. To kill a bad guy you must wield your sword twice. To collect objects walk into them. To start and continue challenges follow the Q coins hovering above ground and enter into the glowing circle below them.
